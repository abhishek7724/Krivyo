What Krivyo does while you work

Six things happen between pressing Start capture and getting a document you can hand to a client.

01

Captures the actual work

Clicks, typed values, dropdown selections and screen changes, each with a timestamp, element label, accessibility attributes and the page it happened on.

02

Rings what you touched

Every interacted element gets a ring drawn on the screenshot, so a reader's eye lands on the right field instead of hunting across a full-page grab.

03

Masks what shouldn't travel

Passwords, email addresses and recognised sensitive fields are masked before the screenshot is stored. Metadata is length-limited and common patterns are redacted.

04

Lets you fix it before anyone sees it

Rename steps, rewrite descriptions, crop a screenshot, move the highlight, drop a step that shouldn't be there. The capture is a draft until you say otherwise.

05

Exports a document, not a dump

A PDF with your document title, your company logo, your client's logo and who prepared it — set once in your profile, applied to every capture after that.

06

Hands over the raw material too

A ZIP containing raw-session.json, process-model.json and numbered screenshots — the structured record everything else gets built from.

Three moves

How a capture becomes a document

  1. 01

    Start capture

    Open the side panel and press Start capture. Nothing is recorded before this — not a click, not a screenshot.

  2. 02

    Do the work

    Run the process exactly as you would demonstrate it. Pause when you need to fetch something, resume when you're back. Krivyo builds the step timeline as you go.

  3. 03

    Review, then export

    Read the steps back, tidy the wording, crop anything that shouldn't be in frame, then download the PDF or the full ZIP.

Why security teams sign off

Your capture stays on your machine

Consultants record production screens. That only works if the data doesn't wander.

Recording is explicit

Capture begins when you press the button and ends when you stop it. There is no background listening.

Storage is local

Events and the process model sit in extension session storage. Screenshots go to the extension's local IndexedDB on your device.

Nothing uploads on its own

Screenshots and entered values are never sent automatically. Issue-report attachments travel only when you tick them yourself.

No sale, no ads, no brokers

User data is not sold, not used for personalised advertising and not shared with data brokers.
